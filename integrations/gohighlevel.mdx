Connecting GoHighLevel lets new contacts start a WhatsApp conversation automatically, offers your GoHighLevel calendars on the appointment step, and writes the outcome of each conversation back onto the contact.

The integration is offered in the client accounts an agency manages (see [Agency accounts](/integrations/agency)). Find it under **Integrations**, in **Automations**.

<Note>
    In an agency's client accounts the dashboard does not name the vendor: the card and the page are called **CRM**, and the page lives at `/integrations/crm`. Everything on this page applies the same way.
</Note>

## Connecting

The app is installed from the GoHighLevel Marketplace. An agency installs it **once**, and GoHighLevel asks which sub-accounts to put it on.

<Steps>
    <Step title="Install from the Marketplace">
        Choose the sub-accounts Whappy should run. You can add the app to more sub-accounts later, in GoHighLevel.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Land back in Whappy">
        If you are not signed in, Whappy asks you to **Sign in** or **Create an account**. The installation stays valid for 60 minutes.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm">
        Whappy asks **Connect this installation?** and names the agency or sub-account and your account. Click **Connect** only if you started the installation yourself.
    </Step>
</Steps>

For an agency install, every sub-account that carries the app becomes a client account in Whappy on its own, and appears under **Agency → Clients**. There is nothing to activate by hand. A sub-account you add the app to later appears too.

With the app:

- Webhooks are set up automatically: there is no URL or secret to copy.
- The connection renews itself, so it does not expire.
- Inbound webhooks are signed by GoHighLevel.

Whappy reads each client's contacts, calendars and custom fields.

<Note>
    Your plan for each client is chosen and charged **in GoHighLevel**. Changing a client's plan in the Marketplace moves their limits in Whappy: there is nothing to change here.
</Note>

<Note>
    The page can also connect a single sub-account with a Location ID and a Private Integration Token instead of the app. That option is shown only when the dashboard names GoHighLevel, so in an agency's client accounts it is hidden. Use the Marketplace install.
</Note>

## What connecting gives you

- **New contacts start a WhatsApp conversation automatically.**
- **No duplicates.** Contacts are matched by their GoHighLevel id, so running a workflow again never creates a second lead.
- **Routing by tag.** Send contacts to different campaigns based on their GoHighLevel tags.
- **Calendars on the appointment step.** Your GoHighLevel calendars can be picked on the appointment step.

Once connected, the page has three tabs: **Connection**, **Contacts** and **Results**.

## Connection

Shows the connected sub-account. If an agency set up the connection, the tab says your agency manages it and there is nothing to configure; use **Contacts** and **Results**.

If GoHighLevel refuses a change Whappy sends, the tab shows **Needs attention**: results or bookings are not reaching GoHighLevel, usually because the connection is missing a permission. Check its permissions in GoHighLevel, then connect again.

If the app was removed from the sub-account in GoHighLevel, the page says so: reinstall it from the Marketplace. Routing and field mappings are kept.

## Contacts: which contacts Whappy writes to

Whappy reads the tags on your GoHighLevel contacts. Under **Which contacts**, choose:

- **Only contacts with a tag below.** Other contacts are ignored. If you add one of these tags later, the contact comes in then.
- **Every new contact.** Contacts with a tag below follow its rule; all others go to your default campaign.

Under **Tag rules**, click **Add a rule**, type a tag and pick a **Campaign**. If a contact has two of these tags, the rule higher in the list wins: reorder them with **Move up** and **Move down**. A tag added after the first message does not move the contact. Click **Save**.

A contact whose campaign does not talk on WhatsApp is not brought in (see [Channel](/campaign/channel)).

## Results: writing back to GoHighLevel

Turn on **Send results to GoHighLevel**. Whappy updates the contact when a conversation ends, a person takes over, the lead opts out, or the lead stays silent for 24 hours. The contact gets:

- A **note** with the conversation.
- A **tag** with the result: `whappy-closed`, `whappy-human-takeover`, `whappy-no-reply` or `whappy-opted-out`.
- The **answers you link**, in your own fields.
- The **deal moved** in your pipeline, if you choose one.

Click **Save** when you are done.

### Answers to GoHighLevel fields

Click **Link an answer**, pick a **Whappy answer** and the **GoHighLevel field** it should fill. Answers you do not link stay in Whappy. The note and the tag are sent either way.

If the tab says no contact custom fields were found, create them in GoHighLevel first, then reopen the tab.

### Pipeline

Choose a **Pipeline** to move the deal when the conversation ends, then a **Stage** for **When the conversation finishes** and for **When a person takes over**. **Leave the stage as it is** skips one of them.

Leave the pipeline on **Don't create or move deals** and your pipeline is never touched. This is the default.

## If the install fails

| Message | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Could not start the GoHighLevel install** | The app credentials are not configured. Contact support. |
| **Authorization denied or an error occurred with GoHighLevel** | The install was declined in GoHighLevel, or GoHighLevel returned an error. Try again. |
| **Missing required parameters for the GoHighLevel installation** | The callback arrived incomplete. Start the install again. |
| **This installation expired** | More than 60 minutes passed. Install the app again from the Marketplace. |
| **This link doesn't match the GoHighLevel connection started in this browser** | Nothing was connected. Start the connection again from **Integrations**. |

In an agency's client accounts these messages say **CRM** instead of GoHighLevel.
