Connect Whappy to Zapier to start a Zap when something happens in a conversation, and to add leads to Whappy from forms, ads, sheets or any other app on Zapier.

Zapier is on the **Integrations** page, under **Automations**. Every account sees it except an agency's client accounts, which use their agency's CRM instead. [Make](/integrations/make) offers the same events and action.

## What it does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
    <Card title="Whappy starts your Zaps when" icon="arrow-right">
        - **Conversation closed**: a conversation reaches its Close step.
        - **Appointment booked**: a lead books a meeting.
        - **Lead went quiet**: a lead stops replying for the hours you set in the Zap.
        - **Send data**: a Send data step runs in a campaign funnel.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Your Zaps can" icon="arrow-left">
        - **Create lead**: add a lead to the Whappy campaign you choose.
    </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connect

<Steps>
    <Step title="Install the Whappy app on Zapier">
        In Whappy, open **Integrations → Zapier** and click **Get Whappy app on Zapier**. Zapier opens the Whappy app page.
    </Step>

    <Step title="Copy your API key">
        On the same page, click **Copy API key** next to the key. Paste it when Zapier asks you to connect your Whappy account.

        <img src="/images/zapier/copy-api-key.png" alt="The Zapier API key in Whappy" />

        <img src="/images/zapier/paste-api-key.png" alt="Pasting the API key in Zapier" />

        <Note>
            One key covers both directions: triggers and Create lead. It is separate from your Make key; revoking one never stops the other. Keep it private: anyone with the key can add leads to your account.
        </Note>
    </Step>
</Steps>

Below the steps, the page shows Zapier's ready-made templates for Whappy. Pick one to start a Zap that is already set up.

## Add leads to Whappy (Create lead)

<Steps>
    <Step title="Set up the app the leads come from">
        Create a Zap and choose your lead source as the trigger: a form, an ad platform, a sheet, a CRM.
    </Step>

    <Step title="Add Whappy as the action">
        Search for **Whappy** and choose **Create Lead** as the action event. Connect your Whappy account with the API key if you have not yet.

        <div style={{ textAlign: 'center' }}>
            <img src="/images/zapier/setup-action.png" alt="Choosing Create Lead in Zapier" style={{ maxWidth: '350px', width: '100%', height: 'auto' }} />
        </div>
    </Step>

    <Step title="Map the lead's fields">
        - The phone number, in international format, e.g. `+393331234567`. A local number works if your account has a default country.
        - The name.
        - Optional: the email, the Whappy campaign the lead goes to (left empty, your account's default routing decides), and extra lead data such as budget or city, which the AI and your templates can read.
        - Mark the lead as a test lead while you build the Zap.

        <div style={{ textAlign: 'center' }}>
            <img src="/images/zapier/configure-action.png" alt="Mapping lead fields in Zapier" style={{ maxWidth: '350px', width: '100%', height: 'auto' }} />
        </div>
    </Step>

    <Step title="Test and turn it on">
        Click **Test action**, then check the lead in the campaign's **Leads** tab in Whappy. If it is there, turn the Zap on.

        <div style={{ textAlign: 'center' }}>
            <img src="/images/zapier/test-action.png" alt="Testing the action in Zapier" style={{ maxWidth: '350px', width: '100%', height: 'auto' }} />
        </div>
    </Step>
</Steps>

What happens next:

- **The same person again.** If the lead is already in that campaign, Whappy keeps one lead and adds the new details. If the campaign has **Restart the campaign for a lead that comes back** on (campaign **Options**), the conversation starts over.
- **A campaign that does not talk on WhatsApp** refuses the lead: a lead from Zapier has a phone number, and the campaign writes first on WhatsApp. Turn WhatsApp on in the campaign's [channels](/campaign/channel), or pick another campaign. Test leads are not refused.
- **No WhatsApp number yet.** Whappy writes first on WhatsApp only. On an account with no WhatsApp number connected, the lead waits until one is.

## Start Zaps from Whappy (triggers)

<Steps>
    <Step title="Choose the Whappy trigger">
        Create a Zap, search for **Whappy** as the trigger app and pick the event you want.

        <img src="/images/zapier/search-whappy.png" alt="Searching for Whappy in Zapier" />
    </Step>

    <Step title="Fill in the trigger's fields">
        - **Lead went quiet** asks for the hours without a reply. Each lead fires once. If you have several of these Zaps, the shortest wait applies to all of them.
        - **Send data** asks for a trigger name. Use the same name in the campaign's Send data step (see below).
    </Step>

    <Step title="Test the trigger">
        Click **Test trigger**. Zapier loads a sample lead so you can map its fields in the next steps.

        <div style={{ textAlign: 'center' }}>
            <img src="/images/zapier/test-trigger.png" alt="Testing the trigger in Zapier" style={{ maxWidth: '350px', width: '100%', height: 'auto' }} />
        </div>
    </Step>

    <Step title="Add the app the data goes to and turn the Zap on">
        Choose the app (a CRM, a sheet, an email tool), map the Whappy fields and turn the Zap on.
    </Step>
</Steps>

### Send data steps

**Send data** fires only where you put it. In the campaign's funnel, add a **Send data** step, choose **Zapier or Make** as the **Connection type**, and pick the **Trigger name** you set in Zapier. If the list is empty, create the trigger in Zapier first. Add the step to every campaign that should send the data.

### What each event sends

Every trigger sends the same fields:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `lead_id` | Whappy's id of the lead. |
| `name`, `phone` | The lead. `phone` is empty for a lead with no phone number, such as an Instagram lead. |
| `campaign_name` | The lead's campaign. |
| `created_at` | When the lead was created. |
| `appointment` → `full_date` | The booked date and time, or empty. |
| `lead_info_json` | What the lead arrived with (form fields, lead data). |
| `collected_info_json` | What the AI collected in the conversation, by field name. |
| `conversation_json` | The messages, each with `sender` (`ai`, `lead`, or `user` for your team), `message` and `datetime`. |
| `appointment_json` | The appointment's `datetime` and `event_id`. |

The four `*_json` fields arrive as JSON text. In a **Send data** event they arrive as readable text instead (for example `{'Budget': '5000'}`), which is not valid JSON. The [developer reference](/integrations/zapier-developer) has a full example.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Zapier cannot connect to Whappy">
        Copy the key again from **Integrations → Zapier**, with no spaces before or after it. Make sure it is the Zapier key, not the Make key.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="A trigger never fires">
        - Check the Zap is **on**.
        - Check leads actually reach the step the trigger listens to: the Close step, a booked appointment, or the Send data step.
        - **Send data**: the trigger name in the funnel step must be the one in the Zap.
        - **Lead went quiet**: only a conversation waiting for the lead's reply counts, and each lead fires once.
        - Look at the Zap's history in Zapier for errors from the app the data goes to.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Create Lead fails">
        - **The phone number cannot be read**: send it with the international prefix, or set a default country in Whappy.
        - **The campaign does not talk on WhatsApp**: turn WhatsApp on in the campaign's channels, or send the lead to another campaign.
        - **Unknown campaign name**: the name must match a Whappy campaign; otherwise your account's default routing decides.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="A lead is missing information">
        Check the field mapping in the Zap, and that the source app sends the fields you mapped. Extra fields go in the lead data.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
