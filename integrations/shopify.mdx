Connect your Shopify store and Whappy's AI talks to your customers on WhatsApp at the moments that matter: a cart left behind, an order paid, an order shipped, an order delivered. It answers questions about their orders and your products from your store's own data, and it can suggest a related product.

## Connecting your store

1. Install the **Whappy** app in your Shopify admin.
2. Open it from **Apps → Whappy**. Click **Get started** to create your Whappy account from your Shopify login, or **Connect** if you already have one with the same email.
3. Click **Open Whappy** to go to your dashboard. Your store's page is **Integrations → Shopify**.

To link the store to a Whappy account with a different email, click **Use a different account**. Whappy opens in a new tab: sign in with that account, check the store and the account, and click **Link store**.

Plans for a Shopify store are billed through Shopify. If you paid Whappy by card before, linking your store cancels that plan at once and refunds the days you had already paid for; then choose your plan from **Apps → Whappy** in your Shopify admin.

### What changes for a store's account

Once linked, the account is a Shopify store's account for good, even if you disconnect the store later:

- Your plan is billed through Shopify. **Integrations → Shopify** shows it under **Plan**; change it from Whappy in your Shopify admin.
- No CRM integration.
- No lead imports: your customers come from the store. You can still add test leads by hand.
- No agency features.

An agency's account, or an account an agency manages, cannot link a store. Neither can an account with a CRM connected: disconnect it first.

## Who becomes a lead

Only customers who **agreed to WhatsApp marketing** in your store become leads and receive messages. You can see and change a customer's WhatsApp marketing status on their customer page in Shopify.

- When a customer agrees to WhatsApp marketing, they become a lead. Their name, email and phone come from your store.
- When they withdraw it, they stop receiving marketing messages from Whappy.
- A customer who sends **STOP** on WhatsApp is never messaged again by a store event, even if their status in Shopify says otherwise.
- SMS and email marketing consent do not count.

<Note>
    Shopify's checkout asks only for SMS consent, not WhatsApp consent. An abandoned cart is followed up only for customers who already agreed to WhatsApp marketing in your store.
</Note>

## Your store's campaigns

When you connect your store, Whappy creates five campaigns for you, switched off so you can review them first:

| Campaign | Starts when | Waits before the first message |
|---|---|---|
| Abandoned checkout | A checkout is left unfinished | 1 hour (at least 15 minutes) |
| Order paid | An order is paid | No wait |
| Order shipped | An order is fulfilled | No wait |
| Order delivered | A shipment is delivered | 1 day |
| Customer support | A customer writes to you first | No wait |

On **Integrations → Shopify**, **Your campaigns** lists the five campaigns, the event that starts each one, and whether it is **On** or **Off**. While Whappy is still preparing them, it says so; this takes a minute or two.

Each event campaign's first message is a WhatsApp template approved by Meta. Under **Your campaigns**, click **Prepare message templates**: Whappy drafts one template per event campaign and sets it as that campaign's first message. It needs a WhatsApp number connected first. Then click **Review templates**: the drafts open on **Integrations → WhatsApp**, where you check them and send them to Meta for approval. After the first message, the AI helps the customer and closes the conversation once they have nothing more to ask. You can edit every campaign like any other: all funnel steps are available, and you can connect Calendly or Cal.com to book appointments.

If a later event reaches a customer who is already in that campaign (for example "shipped" after "paid"), the campaign starts over for them, unless they exchanged messages within the campaign's quiet hours.

### Store events

**Store events** on **Integrations → Shopify** sets which campaign each store event starts: **New customer**, **Abandoned checkout**, **Order paid**, **Order shipped** and **Order delivered**. Each event starts one campaign, and one campaign can start on several events. Pick **No campaign** to start nothing on that event. You can also set it under **Starts when** in the campaign's **Options**. The abandoned-checkout campaign must wait at least 15 minutes before its first message: set its start delay on its **Options** page first.

**Create leads from new customers** decides whether a new customer who agreed to WhatsApp marketing becomes a lead. When it is off, checkouts and orders still start their campaigns.

A store event only starts a campaign that talks on WhatsApp (see [Channel](/campaign/channel)). If the event's campaign does not, the event starts nothing; a new customer becomes a lead with no campaign.

### Order details in your messages

Templates and AI messages can use the latest order's details: **Insert variable** in the template editor lists them.

| Variable | Example |
|---|---|
| `{{shopify_order_number}}` | #1042 |
| `{{shopify_order_url}}` | The order page, which shows the tracking |
| `{{shopify_order_total}}` | 59.00 EUR |
| `{{shopify_order_items}}` | 2 × Linen shirt, 1 × Belt |
| `{{shopify_checkout_url}}` | The link that reopens an abandoned cart |

## Chat button on your store

Add a WhatsApp button to your storefront. A visitor who taps it opens WhatsApp (the app on a phone, WhatsApp Web on a computer) with a greeting ready to send to your number. On a product page the greeting names the product, for example "Hi My Store! I'm looking at Linen shirt", so the AI knows what they were looking at. The AI answers in **Customer support** and can answer product questions straight away.

### Switching it on

1. Connect a WhatsApp number in Whappy first: the button links to it.
2. On **Integrations → Shopify**, switch on **Chat button on your store**. Whappy starts Customer support and sends the number's incoming messages to it.
3. Click **Activate in your theme**. Your theme editor opens with the button switched on: click **Save**.

To switch it on in your theme yourself instead of step 3:

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Themes**.
2. Click **Customize** on your current theme.
3. Open **App embeds** (the icon in the left sidebar).
4. Turn on **WhatsApp chat button**.
5. Click **Save**.

<Note>
    Everyone who writes to that WhatsApp number reaches Customer support, not only the visitors who tap the button.
</Note>

### What you can change

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Whappy, **Integrations → Shopify** | On or off; WhatsApp logo or your own logo; the greeting; the greeting on product pages (`{product}` is replaced by the product's name) |
| Shopify theme editor, **App embeds → WhatsApp chat button** | Position, colour, button text, your logo's image, every page or product pages only, distance from the bottom |

Switching the button off in Whappy removes it from your store within a few minutes; Customer support keeps running. Visitors who arrive through the button are tagged **SHOPIFY_WIDGET** in your leads.

## What the AI knows about your store

When you connect your store, Whappy turns it into knowledge sources on each campaign's **Context** page (called **Knowledge** in Customer support): the store's name, web address, currency and time zone; its home page; up to 50 of your active products with their description, price and product page; and your policies. A home page behind a password, as before launch, is skipped. From these sources Whappy writes the **Business Context** that every one of the five campaigns uses.

These are ordinary knowledge sources: add your own and press **Update context** like in any campaign (see [Context](/campaign/context)). A Business Context you edited is never replaced by Whappy. The products source is a copy taken when you connected; the AI still looks up prices and products live when a customer asks (see below).

## Answers about orders

When a customer asks "where is my order?", the AI answers from your store's record of their recent orders: number, date, items, total, status, carrier and tracking, and the order page. It never invents an order or a tracking number; if it cannot see the order, it says so and points the customer to you.

## Answers about products and suggestions

The AI knows your catalog.

- **Product questions.** When a customer asks about a product ("do you have it in blue?", "how much is the large one?"), the AI looks it up in your store and answers with the price and a link to the product. It quotes only what your store lists, and says so when it cannot find something.
- **Suggestions.** After answering, the AI may suggest **one** product related to what the customer bought or has in their cart, with a link to buy it in one tap. The related products come from Shopify's own recommendations, so there is nothing to set up. It never suggests anything when the customer has a problem, and never again after they decline. With an abandoned cart, it helps the customer finish the cart first.

Customers who opted out of marketing never get suggestions; their product questions are still answered.

### Turning suggestions off

Suggestions are on for every campaign. To turn them off for one campaign, open the campaign's **Options** and switch off **Suggest products from your store**. Product questions are answered either way.

<Note>
    When Shopify is very busy, a reply may arrive a little later, or without product details. The AI always replies.
</Note>

## Your customers' data

Whappy reads your store and never changes its data; the only thing it writes is the chat button's settings, which your theme reads. It uses your customers' name, email and phone, their recent orders and open carts, and your catalog; it does not use addresses. When a customer asks Shopify for their data or for its deletion, Whappy handles the request automatically. See the [privacy policy](https://whappy.ai/legal/privacy) and the [data processing addendum](https://whappy.ai/legal/dpa).

## Disconnecting

To stop creating leads from your store, click **Disconnect** at the end of **Integrations → Shopify** and confirm. The chat button disappears from your store. Open Whappy again in your Shopify admin to reconnect.

To remove Whappy completely, uninstall the Whappy app from your Shopify admin. Whappy stops receiving store events at once; your store's data is deleted when Shopify sends its deletion request, 48 hours after the uninstall.
