Once Whappy is [connected to your assistant](/integrations/mcp) through an MCP connector, you talk to it in plain language. These prompts work in any connected assistant; change the details to fit your business.

## Building a campaign

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Build an agent from your website">
    > Build me a WhatsApp agent for example.com. It should qualify people who fill in our contact form and book a 20-minute call.

    The assistant reads the site, writes the campaign's knowledge, the questions it asks and the funnel, checks the campaign's health and tells you what is left to do in the dashboard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Improve the questions it asks">
    > In my "Dental clinic" campaign, add a question asking whether the patient has dental insurance, and only offer a booking to people who say yes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rehearse before going live">
    > Rehearse my "Dental clinic" campaign against three invented patients: one in a hurry, one who only wants prices, and one who asks for a discount. Tell me where the agent struggled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Draft a WhatsApp template">
    > Draft a WhatsApp template in English and Italian that reminds a lead about their appointment tomorrow, and submit it to Meta.

    The assistant drafts and translates it and submits it to Meta when you say so. With **Let it act in my chats**, it can also send a test to a number you give it, never to one of your contacts: "Send me a test on +39 333 1234567.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reviewing your inbox

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Inbox summary">
    > Give me my inbox summary: unread, waiting, stopped and failed chats in each campaign.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happened overnight">
    > Which conversations from the last 24 hours ended without a booking? Summarise why for each one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Find one contact">
    > Find the contact called Marco Rossi and tell me what he asked and where the conversation stopped.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reports

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Weekly results">
    > How did my campaigns do this week compared with last week? Show leads, replies and booked calls per campaign in a table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Coach's advice">
    > What does the Coach say about my "Dental clinic" campaign? Which of its proposals would have the most impact?
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Working the inbox

These work on every connection made since 22 November 2026; an older one has to [reconnect](/integrations/ai-troubleshooting#the-assistant-says-to-reconnect) first.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Take over a chat">
    > Pause the AI in the chat with Marco Rossi: I will answer him myself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tidy up after reading">
    > Mark the chats you just summarised as read, and tag everyone who asked about prices with "pricing".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fix a contact">
    > Marco Rossi writes in German: set his language to German. And Laura Bianchi asked not to get offers any more: take her off marketing messages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Replying to contacts

<Note>
  Only for connections where you ticked **Let it act in my chats**. See [Acting in your chats](/integrations/mcp#acting-in-your-chats).
</Note>

> Reply to Marco Rossi that we can see him on Thursday at 10:00, and ask whether that works.

The assistant first prepares the message: Whappy returns who it goes to (masked), the channel and the exact text, and nothing is sent until the assistant confirms it. Most assistants show you that preview and ask before confirming.

> Turn the AI back on in the chat with Marco Rossi.

## Tips

- **Name the campaign** when you have more than one, so the assistant does not have to guess.
- **Ask for a table** when you want numbers side by side.
- **Say "don't change anything yet"** when you only want a suggestion. The assistant will describe the change instead of making it.
- Switching a campaign on, deleting campaigns or contacts, importing contacts and billing always happen in the [dashboard](https://app.whappy.ai): the assistant will tell you so.
