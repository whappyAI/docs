Once Whappy is [connected to your assistant](/integrations/mcp), you talk to it in plain language. These prompts work in any connected assistant; change the details to fit your business.

## Building a campaign

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Build an agent from your website">
    > Build me a WhatsApp agent for example.com. It should qualify people who fill in our contact form and book a 20-minute call.

    The assistant reads the site, writes the campaign's knowledge, the questions it asks and the funnel, checks the campaign's health and tells you what is left to do in the dashboard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Improve the questions it asks">
    > In my "Dental clinic" campaign, add a question asking whether the patient has dental insurance, and only offer a booking to people who say yes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rehearse before going live">
    > Rehearse my "Dental clinic" campaign against three invented patients: one in a hurry, one who only wants prices, and one who asks for a discount. Tell me where the agent struggled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Draft a WhatsApp template">
    > Draft a WhatsApp template in English and Italian that reminds a lead about their appointment tomorrow, and send me a test on +39 333 1234567.

    The assistant drafts and translates it, submits it to Meta when you say so, and sends the test only to the number you give it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reviewing your inbox

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happened overnight">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Find one lead">

## Reports

## Tips

- **Name the campaign** when you have more than one, so the assistant does not have to guess.
- **Ask for a table** when you want numbers side by side.
- **Say "don't change anything yet"** when you only want a suggestion. The assistant will describe the change instead of making it.
- Starting a campaign, adding leads and billing always happen in the [dashboard](https://app.whappy.ai): the assistant will tell you so.
