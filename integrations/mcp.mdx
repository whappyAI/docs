Whappy can be attached to an AI assistant that speaks the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Once connected, you describe what you want in the chat and the assistant works in your Whappy account: "build me a WhatsApp agent for example.com" is enough to get a funnel, the questions it asks, and its knowledge of your business. You can also ask it how your campaigns are doing and what your leads said.

The connection is yours to grant and yours to withdraw. Nothing happens until you allow it, and everything it does happens inside your account under your name.

## Connect your assistant

Every assistant uses the same address:

```
https://mcp.whappy.ai/mcp
```

## What happens when you connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Whappy in your assistant">
    Give your assistant the address above. Each client's page says where.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Whappy">
    The assistant opens a Whappy page in your browser. Sign in with the account you want it to work in, or create one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow the connection">
    The consent page shows which assistant is asking, the account you are signed in with, and where you will be sent back. Click **Allow**, or **Deny**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start working">
    Back in your assistant, say what you want. A good first message is a website address and who you want to reach.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The consent page is valid for 10 minutes. If it says the request has expired, start again from your assistant.

<Note>
  Coming soon: the consent page will also offer one box, **Let it act in my chats**, unticked by default. Tick it only if you want the assistant to be able to message your leads (see [Acting in your chats](#acting-in-your-chats)). Today there is nothing to tick: **Allow** gives every connection the same tools, listed below.
</Note>

## What a connection can do

### With Allow

A connected assistant can:

- Build a campaign from a website or a description of the business, and create, rename, duplicate or delete campaigns.
- Read, generate and edit the funnel.
- Write the campaign's knowledge and the questions it collects.
- Draft WhatsApp templates, translate them, submit them to Meta, and send a test template to a phone number you give it.
- Choose which campaign answers a WhatsApp number's incoming messages, and which channels a campaign talks on.
- Change a campaign's working hours, time zone and start delay.
- Rehearse a campaign against invented customers.
- Read your leads, conversations, results and Coach reports, your calendar settings and your plan.

<Note>
  Coming soon, with Allow: an inbox summary (unread, waiting, stopped and failed chats per campaign), reading one conversation page by page, your daily briefing, results by channel, and **pausing the AI** in a chat so you can take it over yourself.
</Note>

### Acting in your chats

<Note>
  Coming soon. Nothing on this section works yet: today a connection cannot message your leads in any way. It will arrive after the first directory listings, which go out without messaging.
</Note>

If you tick **Let it act in my chats** on the consent page, the assistant can also:

- **Reply** in a chat with one of your leads.
- **Send a first message** to a lead who has not been contacted yet.
- **Resume the AI** in a chat that was paused, so your Whappy agent starts answering that lead again.

WhatsApp's own rules still apply: outside WhatsApp's 24-hour customer-service window, only an approved template can be sent.

Every message goes through **two steps**:

1. The assistant asks Whappy for a **preview**: the recipient (masked), the channel and the exact text. It shows you that preview.
2. Only when you say yes does it send. The preview can be used once, for 5 minutes; after that the assistant has to ask for a new one.

Most assistants also ask you to approve the action before they call Whappy at all, so you will usually confirm twice. A connection without the box ticked is refused, and the assistant is told to ask you to reconnect: see [Troubleshooting](/integrations/ai-troubleshooting#a-permission-is-missing).

### Never through a connection

Some things stay in the dashboard by design, whatever you tick:

- **Starting or stopping a campaign.**
- **Bulk sends and broadcasts**, **adding or importing leads**, and **deleting leads**.
- **Billing, subscriptions, API keys and account changes.**
- **Connecting WhatsApp or Instagram.** Meta's sign-up runs in a browser, so the assistant gives you a link and you finish it there.

When an assistant asks for something outside its reach, Whappy refuses and says it happens in the dashboard. The assistant inside the Whappy dashboard can do more than a connection.

## Limits

These bound what can happen while you are not watching.

| Limit | Value | Counted |
|---|---|---|
| AI generation requests (building campaigns, generating funnels, rehearsals) | 30 per hour | per account, shared with the in-app assistant and every connection |
| New client registrations | 20 per hour | per network address. You will not meet this one unless something is misbehaving. |

When a limit is reached, Whappy says so and how long to wait. See [Troubleshooting](/integrations/ai-troubleshooting#too-many-requests-429).

<Note>
  Coming soon: every connection will also be limited to **60 tool calls a minute and 300 an hour**. When messaging arrives, it comes with its own caps:

  | Limit | Value |
  |---|---|
  | Messages to leads (replies, first messages, templates) | 30 per hour per connection, and 150 per day per account |
  | First messages | 20 per day per account |
  | WhatsApp templates | 20 per day per account |
  | Resuming the AI | 60 per hour per connection |
</Note>

## The data an assistant sees

A connection reads what it needs to do its job: your campaigns, and your leads and their conversations when you ask about them. What the assistant reads is also processed by its provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI or whoever makes your client) under that provider's terms.

Today, lead names, phone numbers and email addresses are returned in full.

<Note>
  Coming soon: lead phone numbers and email addresses will be **masked by default**, for example `+39 ••• ••• 1234`. Searching still works on the full number, so "find +39 333 1234567" finds the lead without the number coming back in full. An account setting will let you share full contact details with your connected assistants; it will be off unless you turn it on.
</Note>

A connection never sees your Meta access tokens, your API keys or your card details.

## Staying connected

Your assistant is given access for one hour at a time and renews it quietly in the background for up to 30 days. If you do not use the connection for 30 days, you will be asked to sign in again.

## Revoking a connection

Remove Whappy in your assistant. Each client's page says how. Clients that support it tell Whappy to revoke the access immediately, and it cannot be renewed.

<Note>
  Coming soon: a **Connected apps** page in the Whappy dashboard listing every connection, since when it is connected and what it did lately, with a button to disconnect it. Until then, disconnect from the assistant's side. If you have lost access to the assistant, or you believe a connection has been compromised, [contact support](/support/contact-support) and we will revoke it for you.
</Note>

## If you are on an agency account

Connecting an assistant to a **client account you manage** is not supported yet. A connection is bound to the person who approved it and acts in their own account, so switching into a managed account will not carry over to it.
