Whappy can be attached to Claude, or to any other client that speaks the Model Context Protocol, as a **custom connector**. Once connected, you describe what you want in the chat and it builds the campaign in your Whappy account: "build me a WhatsApp agent for example.com" is enough to get a funnel, the questions it asks, and its knowledge of your business.

The connection is yours to grant and yours to withdraw. Nothing happens until you allow it, and everything it does happens inside your account under your name.

## Connecting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the connector">
    In your client, add a custom connector with this URL:

    ```
    https://mcp.whappy.ai/mcp
    ```

    In Claude, that is **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector**. Whappy is not in the connector directory, so it has to be added by URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Whappy">
    The client opens a Whappy page in your browser. Sign in with the account you want the client to work in, or create one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what it may do">
    You will see who is asking and a choice of what to allow: **Read only**, **Draft** or **Build**. Pick one and click **Allow**, or **Deny**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start building">
    Back in the client, say what you want. A good first message is a website address and who you want to reach.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can allow

The consent page shows which client is asking, the account you are signed in with, and where you will be sent back. It asks **What may this connection do?** and offers **Read only**, **Draft** or **Build**, and says that a connection never starts campaigns, messages people or changes billing. Click **Allow** or **Deny**.

Today the level you pick does not change what the connection can do: every connection gets the same tools, listed below. **Launch** and **Operate** are never offered to a connection.

The consent page is valid for 10 minutes. If it says the request has expired, start again from your client.

## What a connected client can and cannot do

A connected client can:

- Build a campaign from a website or a description of the business, and create, rename, duplicate or delete campaigns.
- Read, generate and edit the funnel.
- Write the campaign's knowledge and the questions it collects.
- Draft WhatsApp templates, translate them, submit them to Meta, and send a test template to a phone number.
- Choose which campaign answers a WhatsApp number's incoming messages.
- Change a campaign's working hours, time zone and start delay.
- Rehearse a campaign against invented customers.
- Read your leads, conversations, results and Coach reports, your calendar settings and your plan.

Some things stay out of its reach by design:

- **Starting or stopping a campaign.** You do that in the dashboard.
- **Messaging your leads** and **adding leads.**
- **Billing, subscriptions and account changes.**
- **Connecting WhatsApp or Instagram.** Meta's sign-up runs in a browser, so the client gives you a link and you finish it there.

When a client asks for something outside this list, Whappy refuses and says it happens in the dashboard. The assistant inside the Whappy dashboard can do more than a connection.

## Limits

These bound what can happen while you are not watching. They are per account, not per connection.

- **30 AI generation requests per hour.** Building campaigns, generating funnels and rehearsals count. The in-app assistant and every connection share the same limit. When it is reached, wait for the hour to roll over and try again.
- **20 new client registrations per hour** from one network address. You will not meet this one unless something is misbehaving.

## Staying connected

Your client is given access for one hour at a time and renews it quietly in the background for up to 30 days. If you do not use the connection for 30 days, you will be asked to sign in again.

## Disconnecting

Remove the connector in your client — Claude calls it **Disconnect**. That tells Whappy to revoke the access immediately, and the client cannot renew it.

<Note>
  There is not yet a page in the Whappy dashboard listing your connected clients. Until there is, disconnect from the client side. If you have lost access to the client, or you believe a connection has been compromised, [contact support](/support/contact-support) and we will revoke it for you.
</Note>

## If you are on an agency account

Connecting a client to a **client account you manage** is not supported in this release. A connection is bound to the person who approved it and acts in their own account, so switching into a managed account will not carry over to it.
