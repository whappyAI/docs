An agency account runs Whappy for its clients from one place: the **Agency** page in the sidebar. Each client is a GoHighLevel sub-account with the Whappy app installed, and gets its own Whappy account.

## Creating your agency

Installing the app as an agency from the GoHighLevel Marketplace creates the agency for you (see [GoHighLevel](/integrations/gohighlevel)). You can also open **Agency** and fill in **Create your agency**: an **Agency name** and a **Billing currency**. The currency cannot be changed later.

A Shopify store's account cannot run an agency.

## Clients

Your clients are your GoHighLevel sub-accounts that carry the app. Each one appears under **Clients** on its own once the app is on it; there is no button to add a client by hand. Plans and invoices are set in GoHighLevel's Marketplace.

Above the list, Whappy tells you when:

- sub-accounts in GoHighLevel have no app yet. Install it where Whappy should run, and they appear here.
- a sub-account has the app but is not set up yet. Try again in a few minutes.
- clients have nothing running yet (**Nothing running** on their row). Use the row menu to copy a campaign.

The list shows each client's **Plan**, **Status** (**Active** or **Suspended**), **Access** (**Client signs in** or **Agency only**) and **Billing** (**On invoice** or **Not billed yet**).

### Working inside a client

Click **Enter** on a client's row. The dashboard switches to that client's account and a banner shows **Managing:** and the client's name. Click **Back to agency** to leave.

Inside a client, **Integrations** offers WhatsApp, Instagram, the CRM, Meta Ads, Webhook and Make. Calendars, Zapier and Shopify are not offered: the client's calendars and automations come from GoHighLevel.

### Row actions

Owners and admins also have a menu on each row:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Give the client access** | Creates a sign-in for the client into the account your team works in. Enter the client's email: it must not already have a Whappy account. Copy the **Access link** and send it; the client sets a password with it. The link is shown once and expires. |
| **Send a new access link** | A fresh link for a client who already has access. |
| **Remove the client's access** | The client can no longer sign in. Your team still can. |
| **Copy a campaign here** | Brings a campaign from another client into this one as a draft. It arrives stopped, without calendar, media or opening line. |
| **Suspend** / **Resume** | A suspended client cannot be entered and its own sign-in is closed. Its data is kept. Resuming does not reopen the client's sign-in: give access again. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the account and everything in it: campaigns, conversations, leads, integrations. This cannot be undone. |

## Team

The **Team** tab lists who works in the agency, as **Owner**, **Admin** or **Member**. Every member can see the clients and enter them. Owners and admins also manage clients, billing and the team.

To add someone, enter the email of their Whappy account, pick a **Role** and click **Add**. A removed member loses access to every client account.

## The CRM's name in client accounts

By default, a client account an agency manages does not name GoHighLevel. The integration is called **CRM**, its page lives at `/integrations/crm`, and its messages say "CRM" too. The option to connect with a private token, which walks through GoHighLevel's own settings screens, is hidden.

## Connecting Claude to a client

A [Claude or MCP connection](/integrations/mcp) belongs to the person who approved it and works in their own account. Entering a client account does not carry it over.
