Every campaign has a **Leads** tab listing everyone in it, what state their conversation is in, and whether they have an appointment booked.

## The table

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The lead's name. A lead who asked not to receive promotional messages carries an **Opted out** badge; they can still write to you and the assistant still answers them. |
| **Contact** | The phone number in full international format. A lead who wrote on Instagram shows the Instagram mark and their @username instead. |
| **Email** | If you have one; otherwise *N/A*. |
| **Conversation** | The state of the conversation. |
| **Appointment** | The date and time of the booking, or *No appointment*. |
| **Labels** | Only on campaigns for existing customers: the labels on this customer, editable in place. |
| **Actions** | View conversation, view lead info, delete. |

A number without a country code is shown in red with a warning. Hover it to see the corrected form, because a number without a country code cannot be messaged.

## Conversation states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready** | In the campaign, no message sent yet. |
| **Started** | The first message has gone out. |
| **Active** | A conversation is under way. |
| **Paused** | The conversation has been stopped. |
| **Completed** | The lead reached the end of the funnel. |
| **Error** | Something went wrong on this conversation. |

## Searching and filtering

**Search** matches leads by name, phone or email.

**Filters** narrows the list by:

- **Conversation state** — one of **Active**, **Ready**, **Error**, **Started** or **Qualified** (a completed conversation), or all of them.
- **Date range** — a *from* and a *to* date.

The button shows how many filters are active, the current filters are summarised inside it, and **Clear all** removes them in one go.

Two more filters appear under the search when they apply:

- **All / WhatsApp / Instagram**, on an account that uses both channels.
- **Tags**, once at least one lead carries a label. Picking several shows leads with any of them.

## Looking at one lead

Each row ends with three buttons:

- **View conversation** — the full message history. See [Conversation history](/leads/conversation-history).
- **View lead info** — the contact details, every answer collected from this lead, and the language the assistant writes to them in. See [Lead language](/leads/lead-management#lead-language).
- **Delete lead**.

## Exporting

Open the **⋮** menu and choose **Export to CSV**. It downloads the leads currently listed, including the information collected from them.

<Note>
    Phone numbers must include a country code. Whappy flags a number missing one and suggests the corrected form, because a number without a country code cannot be messaged.
</Note>
