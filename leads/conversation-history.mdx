**Chats** shows every conversation your campaigns are having, on WhatsApp and on Instagram. Open it from the sidebar.

Until your first conversation happens, the page says so and points you to your campaigns.

## Finding a conversation

The list on the left has four controls:

- **Search** by lead. The search runs across all your conversations, not just the page you are on.
- **Campaign**: one campaign, or *All campaigns*.
- **Channel**: *All*, *WhatsApp* or *Instagram*. This control appears only when your account has both channels connected.
- **All / Unread** switch: unread shows only conversations where the lead wrote something nobody has opened yet. Each of those rows carries a count of the unread messages.

Each row shows the lead's name, when the last message arrived, and what it said — a photo or a voice note is named rather than left blank. An Instagram conversation carries the Instagram mark on the lead's avatar; WhatsApp conversations carry no mark. (On an account that only has Instagram, no mark is shown at all.)

Long lists are paged. `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `←` and `→` change page, and `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `K` jumps to the search box.

## Reading a conversation

Select a conversation to see the full exchange. The header shows:

- The lead's **name**, and under it their phone number or, for Instagram, their **@handle**.
- The **status** of the conversation: Ready, Started, Active, Waiting for reply, Processing, Paused, Completed or Error.
- The **campaign** the lead is in, as a link that opens it.
- A chip saying who is answering right now: **AI** or **Human**.
- On an Instagram chat, when the lead last wrote more than 24 hours ago: *Instagram: the assistant replies when they write again.*

The **Info** button opens the lead card: contact details, the **key information** collected so far (every answer, the ones you marked required flagged *Req*, and *Not provided* against the ones still missing), and when the **last message** was. A test lead is marked *Test*.

This card is the fastest way to see why a conversation stalled — the missing required answer is usually the reason.

### What the messages tell you

Besides the text, a message can carry a small label:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Bot icon | Written by the AI assistant. |
| **Template** | A WhatsApp template you sent from Chats. |
| **Sent from the WhatsApp Business app** | Somebody answered from the phone app, not from Whappy. |
| **Sent from Instagram** | Somebody answered from the Instagram app. |
| **Sent by the assistant: …** | The AI chose to attach this file, and why. See [Files the AI may send on its own](/campaign/assets#files-the-ai-may-send-on-its-own). |
| **Voice note · transcribed** | A voice note the lead sent, shown as text. |
| **Commented on your post** | An Instagram comment that reached you through a comment rule — shown as a quoted card, not a message. |
| **Follow-up not sent** / **Reminder not sent** | A quiet line in the thread: Instagram allows automatic messages only within 24 hours of the lead's last message, so this one was held back. |

Images are shown as thumbnails; documents and voice notes as a chip with the file name.

### Not delivered

When WhatsApp reports that a message did not reach the lead, a red line appears under it:

| Line | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| **Not delivered: more than 24 hours since they last wrote.** | The 24-hour window had closed. Only an approved template can be sent now — see below. |
| **Not delivered: WhatsApp couldn't deliver to this number.** | The number is not reachable on WhatsApp. |
| **Not delivered.** | WhatsApp refused it for another reason. |

On Instagram the equivalent line reads *Not sent: …* and names the reason (window closed, Instagram not connected, no conversation to reply in).

## Taking over from the AI

Every conversation is handled either by the **AI** or by a **human**, and the chip in the header shows which.

- **Stop AI conversation** (the square button) hands it to you. The assistant stops replying and the status becomes *Paused*.
- **Continue AI conversation** (the play button) gives it back.

<Warning>
    You can only type in the message box while the AI is stopped, or once the conversation is **Completed**. While the AI is handling the chat, the box is locked — stop the AI first. The same applies to the paperclip.
</Warning>

While the conversation is yours, reply directly. The lead sees one continuous conversation and cannot tell where the handover happened. Replies you send from Whappy are not shown with a label; replies sent from the WhatsApp or Instagram app are.

<Note>
    Taking over is the right move when a lead asks something your funnel was not built for. If it keeps happening, that is worth fixing in the campaign — [Coach](/autopilot/coach) spots exactly this pattern and proposes wording that handles it.
</Note>

## What you can send, and when

Both channels limit when a business may write. Chats applies the rules before you send and says so under the message box.

### On WhatsApp

- For **24 hours** after the lead's last message you can send anything.
- After that, or when the lead has **never written to you** (a lead you contacted first and who has not answered), WhatsApp only accepts an **approved template**. The box says so and offers **Send a template**.
- If the number this chat runs on has been disconnected, nothing can be sent: *This chat's WhatsApp number isn't connected anymore.*

### On Instagram

- For **24 hours** after the lead's last message you can send anything, and so can the assistant.
- From 24 hours to **7 days**, only a person can reply. The box says *Sent as a human reply* — the assistant stays silent until the lead writes again.
- After 7 days, nothing can be sent until the lead writes again.
- A lead who has only **commented on a post** has not opened a conversation yet. You can reply once they answer the private reply the comment rule sent them.
- If Instagram is disconnected, the box is locked until you reconnect it under Integrations.

Test conversations ignore all of this: nothing is sent to anybody.

## Sending a template from Chats

When WhatsApp only allows a template, **Send a template** opens a dialog listing the **approved templates of this chat's number**, with the ones in the lead's language first (*Their language*). Pick one to see it as the lead will see it, including its header file and buttons.

- Whappy fills in what it knows about the lead. Anything it cannot fill is asked for under **Fill in what Whappy doesn't know**; every field is required.
- A template that opens with a file needs that file set under **Integrations → WhatsApp** first.
- A template that needs a value Chats cannot provide (a one-time code, a catalogue, a flow) is listed but cannot be sent from here.
- If the lead has not answered your last message, the dialog warns you: sending another one may be marked as spam.

<Note>
    Sending a template does not need the AI to be stopped. After the template goes out, the conversation waits for the lead's reply; when it arrives, the assistant continues — unless you had stopped it.
</Note>

You can send **one template per conversation every 24 hours**. The box tells you when the next one is allowed.

If the number has no approved templates yet, the dialog says so and links to **Integrations → WhatsApp**, where you create them.

## Sending a file from Chats

The **paperclip** next to the message box sends files from your [Assets](/campaign/assets) library — documents, images and voice notes.

<Steps>
    <Step title="Pick the files">
        Tick up to **5** files. Only files that have finished uploading are listed. The number next to each tick is the order they will be sent in.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add your text, if you want">
        Whatever is in the message box goes out with the first file: as the caption of a document or an image. If the first file is a voice note, your text is sent as a message of its own, before the files.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Send">
        Each file arrives as its own message, in the order you picked. The bubbles in the thread are the confirmation.
    </Step>
</Steps>

- The paperclip is held under the same rules as a typed reply: outside the WhatsApp 24-hour window, or while the AI is handling the chat, it is disabled.
- On **Instagram** the lead receives each file as a message with the file name and a **link** to it.
- An empty library offers a link to Assets; uploading stays on that page.
- If a later file is refused after earlier ones went out, you are told *Only part of the message was sent* and the thread shows what reached the lead.

## When a send is refused

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **More than 24 hours since they last wrote: WhatsApp only allows an approved template now.** | Use **Send a template**. |
| **This chat's WhatsApp number isn't connected anymore.** | The number was disconnected under Integrations. |
| **WhatsApp couldn't deliver to this number.** | The lead's number is not reachable on WhatsApp. |
| **Too many messages right now. Try again in a minute.** | WhatsApp is rate-limiting your number. |
| **WhatsApp isn't responding. Try again shortly.** | A temporary fault on Meta's side. |
| **Only part of the message was sent.** | A multi-line message or a batch of files failed part-way; what went out is in the thread. |
| **Not sent: Instagram doesn't allow a reply more than 7 days after their last message.** | Wait for the lead to write again. |
| **That file isn't in your library anymore.** / **That file is still uploading.** | Pick another file, or try again in a moment. |

## Moving a lead to another campaign

On a **Paused** or **Completed** conversation, the arrows button (**Continue this lead in another campaign**) hands the lead to a different campaign.

Choose the campaign, then what happens when the lead arrives:

- **Send the new campaign's first message** — it sends its own template right away. Not offered on an Instagram conversation, where a business cannot write first.
- **Wait for the lead to write** — moved silently; the new campaign answers when the lead writes.
- **Continue without the template** (on Instagram: *Continue from the next step*) — the new campaign picks up after its first step.

**Carry over what has been collected** keeps the key information already gathered. The move happens on the next scan, about two minutes later; if the target campaign is paused, the lead is not moved until it runs again.
