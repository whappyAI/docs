Each campaign has a **Leads** tab listing every contact in it. Here you can see the state of each conversation, read the message history, check the information the assistant collected, and keep contact details current.

## The leads table

### Lead information displayed

**Contact details:**
- **Name**: the lead's name, with an **Opted out** badge when they asked not to receive promotional messages
- **Contact**: the phone number, or the Instagram @username for a lead who wrote on Instagram
- **Email**: the email address, if you have one
- **Labels**: on campaigns for existing customers only, the labels on this customer

**Status indicators:**
- **Conversation**: Ready, Started, Active, Paused, Completed or Error
- **Appointment**: the date and time of the booking, or *No appointment*

**Actions:**
- **View conversation**: the message history and the information collected
- **View lead info**: the lead's profile, with every answer collected and the language the assistant writes to them in

### Search and filtering

**Search bar:**
- Search by name, phone number or email
- Results update as you type
- Clear the search to see all leads

**Filters:**
- **Conversation state** and a **date range**, from the Filters button
- **All / WhatsApp / Instagram**, on an account that uses both channels
- **Tags**, once at least one lead carries a label

See [Viewing leads](/leads/viewing-leads) for every column, state and filter.

## Adding new leads

Open the **⋮** menu and choose **Add a lead** to add one contact by hand, or **Import from CSV** for a list. See [Adding & managing leads](/leads/lead-management).

### Required information
- **Name** *(required)*
- **Phone number** in full international format *(required)*

### Optional information
- **Email**
- **Additional information**: your own fields with any extra data

### Adding custom information
- Click **Add field** to create a custom field
- Enter the field name and its value
- Useful for storing budget, company size, project details, and anything else the assistant should know

**Example custom fields:**
```
Company: "TechCorp Inc"
Budget: "$10,000"
Industry: "SaaS"
Priority: "High"
```

## Viewing lead details

### Conversation
Click **View conversation** to read the whole exchange between the lead and the assistant, with the information collected along the way. See [Conversation history](/leads/conversation-history).

### Lead info
Click **View lead info** to see the lead's profile:

**Contact details:**
- Name, phone and email
- How the lead is known on each channel, when they are on more than one
- **Language**: the language the assistant writes to them in and where it came from, with **Change** to set it yourself

**Other lead information:**
- Every custom field and every answer collected in conversation

## Lead status indicators

### Conversation status

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready** | In the campaign, no message sent yet |
| **Started** | The first message has gone out |
| **Active** | A conversation is under way |
| **Paused** | The conversation has been stopped |
| **Completed** | The lead reached the end of the funnel |
| **Error** | Something went wrong on this conversation |

### Appointment status
- **Calendar icon and date**: a meeting is booked, shown at that date and time
- **No appointment**: nothing booked yet

## Managing your leads

### Organisation tips
- **Use labels**: on campaigns for existing customers, labels choose who an announcement goes to
- **Regular review**: check conversation progress regularly
- **Follow up**: use the appointment date for meeting preparation
- **Data quality**: keep contact information updated

### Common actions
- **Search**: find a lead quickly by any contact detail
- **Filter**: narrow the list by state, date, channel or label
- **Review conversations**: check qualification progress and how the assistant performs
- **Delete**: one lead from its row, or several at once with **Delete selected**; conversations are kept
- **Export**: **Export to CSV** in the **⋮** menu downloads the leads currently listed

## Pagination

When you have many leads, the pager at the bottom of the tab moves between pages. It is only shown when there is more than one page.

## Best practices

### Lead management
- **Regular monitoring**: check new conversations daily
- **Label consistently**: use the same labels across similar customers
- **Update information**: keep lead data current as you learn more
- **Country codes**: a number without one is flagged in red and cannot be messaged

### Conversation review
- **Check the assistant's performance**: review how well it qualifies leads
- **Identify patterns**: look for common questions or concerns from leads
- **Optimise flows**: use what you learn to improve your funnel
- **Follow up**: act on completed conversations and booked appointments

### Data organisation
- **Meaningful labels**: use labels that help with follow-up and targeting
- **Custom fields**: track information specific to your qualification process
- **Clean data**: remove or correct incomplete or incorrect information

## Lead sources

Leads appear in the table from several sources:
- **Instagram**: people who message you or comment on a post, on a campaign that talks on Instagram
- **Manual addition**: contacts you add with **Add a lead**
- **CSV import**: lists you import
- **API/Webhook**: leads sent from your own systems
- **Integrations**: contacts from connected tools such as Zapier, Make, your CRM, Meta lead ads or Shopify

<Tip>
    Use the search to find a lead quickly before an important call or meeting. It works across names, phone numbers and email addresses.
</Tip>
