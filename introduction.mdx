Whappy is an AI assistant that talks to your leads and customers on **WhatsApp** and **Instagram**. It writes first to new leads on WhatsApp, answers people who write to you on either channel, asks the questions you choose, books meetings, and hands the conversation to you whenever you want.

## How it works

You set Whappy up in **campaigns**. A campaign is one assistant talking to one group of people:

1. **Tell it about your business.** Add your website, documents or notes; Whappy writes the context the assistant reads before every message. See [Context](/campaign/context).
2. **Choose what it asks.** The details to collect from each lead, such as a budget or a timeline. See [Info](/campaign/info).
3. **Design the conversation.** A visual flow of steps: questions, branches, files to send, a meeting to book, data to pass on. See [Funnel](/campaign/funnel).
4. **Connect a channel.** Your WhatsApp Business number, your Instagram account, or both. See [Channel](/campaign/channel).
5. **Bring in leads.** Add them by hand, import a CSV, or connect a form or CRM. On Instagram, they arrive when they write to you.

A second kind of campaign keeps one ongoing conversation with every customer you already have, and sends them your offers. See [Campaigns for existing customers](/campaign/client-campaigns).

## Where you work

- **[Home](/home)**: what needs you, the last 30 days, your campaigns and integrations.
- **[Chats](/leads/conversation-history)**: every conversation, live. Take over from the AI, send a template or a file, and hand back.
- **[Assets](/campaign/assets)**: the PDFs, images and voice notes you send, and that the assistant may send on its own.
- **[Analytics](/analytics)**: where you lose people, and when they answer.
- **Integrations**: calendars ([Calendly](/integrations/calendly), [Cal.com](/integrations/cal), [GoHighLevel](/integrations/gohighlevel)), lead sources and automation ([Webhook](/integrations/webhook), [Zapier](/integrations/zapier), [Make](/integrations/make)), [Meta Ads](/integrations/meta-conversions) and [Shopify](/integrations/shopify).

## Autopilot

[Autopilot](/autopilot/overview) works on your campaigns rather than on your leads. [Coach](/autopilot/coach) reads your finished conversations and proposes improvements. [Rehearsal](/autopilot/rehearsal) tries a campaign on invented customers before real ones arrive. Both are optional pages you switch on per campaign.
