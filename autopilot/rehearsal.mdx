A rehearsal drives six invented customers through your real funnel, one at a time, and reports how the campaign coped. Nothing is sent to anybody, and no third party is contacted.

## Open Rehearsal

Rehearsal is an optional page. Switch it on first: **Campaigns → your campaign → Options → Sections → Rehearsal**. It then appears in the campaign's sidebar as **Rehearsal**. Press **Rehearse** to start a run.

A [client campaign](/campaign/client-campaigns) does not offer Rehearsal.

You can also ask the [Assistant](/autopilot/trust-levels) to rehearse a campaign for you.

## Who gets tried

The six personas are the ways a real conversation goes wrong. Each is filled in for your business, but the cast is always the same:

| Persona | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **Cooperative** | Answers everything. |
| **Evasive** | Guarded, dodges questions. |
| **Price only** | Only wants a price. |
| **Confused** | Misunderstands things. |
| **Wrong language** | Replies in another language. |
| **Impatient** | One-word answers. |

A funnel that only works on the cooperative persona works on a minority of your leads.

## Nothing leaves Whappy

Rehearsal leads are marked as invented, and the mark travels with them through the whole funnel:

- No appointment is booked in your calendar.
- No **Send data** step fires: no webhook, Zapier or Make call, no WhatsApp notification.
- No CRM contact is created or updated.
- No WhatsApp message is sent to a real number.

You can rehearse a fully connected campaign without touching anything it is connected to.

## It runs in the background

A rehearsal takes a few minutes. Each persona waits for a real reply from your funnel at every turn, as a real lead would.

While it runs the page shows **Rehearsing your campaign…**. You can leave the page; the result is stored with the campaign and is there when you come back.

## Reading the result

The page leads with one number: the share of personas that answered everything your funnel asks. Under it, one sentence sums up the run and names the question missed most often.

**Questions they struggled with** ranks your questions by how many personas did not answer them. It is the same signal [Coach](/autopilot/coach) uses on real conversations, available before you have any.

Open **Who we tried** to see each persona. Each ends in one of these states:

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Answered everything">
        The persona completed the funnel. You see how many messages it took.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Stopped, or didn't get a reply in time">
        The persona did not finish. You see after how many messages it stopped, and which questions it never answered. **This is a result about your campaign** and counts toward the score.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Couldn't run">
        Something failed on our side, and the persona never spoke.

        **This is not a result about your campaign.** It is left out of the score, and it lists no unanswered questions, because it was never asked any.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If the whole run fails, the page says **The rehearsal didn't finish.** Press **Rehearse** to try again.

To run a new rehearsal after a change, press **Rehearse** again at the top of the page. The page shows the new run while it works, then its result.
