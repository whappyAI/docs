A rehearsal drives six invented customers through your real funnel, one at a time, and reports how the campaign coped. Nothing is sent to anybody, and no third party is contacted.

Open it from a campaign: **Campaigns → your campaign → Rehearsal**, then press **Rehearse**.

## Who gets tried

The six personas are not six variations of a cooperative customer. They are the ways a real conversation goes wrong:

| Persona | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **Cooperative** | Answers everything. |
| **Evasive** | Guarded, dodges questions. |
| **Price only** | Only wants a price. |
| **Confused** | Misunderstands things. |
| **Wrong language** | Replies in another language. |
| **Impatient** | One-word answers. |

A funnel that only works on the cooperative persona is a funnel that works on a minority of your leads.

## Nothing leaves the building

Rehearsal leads are marked, and that mark travels with them through the whole pipeline. They never reach a third party:

- No appointment is booked in your calendar.
- No webhook fires.
- No CRM contact is created or updated.
- No WhatsApp message is sent to a real number.

You can rehearse a fully connected, production-ready campaign without touching anything it is connected to.

## It runs in the background

A rehearsal takes a few minutes — each persona waits for a genuine reply at every turn, exactly as a real lead would.

The run is queued, not held open on the page. **You can leave the page** and come back; the result is stored against the campaign and will be there when you return. The page shows **Rehearsing your campaign…** while it works.

## Reading the result

Each persona ends in one of three states, and the difference between the last two matters:

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Answered everything">
        The persona completed the funnel. You see how many messages it took.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Stopped, or didn't reply in time">
        The persona did not finish. You see where it stalled and which questions it never answered. **This is a result about your campaign** and counts toward the score.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Couldn't be run">
        Something failed on our side — the persona never spoke at all.

        **This is not a result about your campaign.** It is excluded from the score entirely, and its list of unanswered questions stays empty, because a persona that was never asked a question did not fail to answer it. Reporting an outage of ours as a failing campaign of yours would be worse than reporting nothing.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Below the personas, **questions they struggled with** ranks your questions by how many personas failed to answer them — the same signal [Coach](/autopilot/coach) uses on real conversations, available before you have any.

## It counts toward unattended launches

A passing rehearsal is one of the two conditions that unlock the **Launch** [permission level](/autopilot/trust-levels). The page tells you when a run counted.

The other condition is three campaigns launched by hand without problems.
