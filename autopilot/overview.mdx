Autopilot is the part of Whappy that works on your campaigns rather than on your leads. It rehearses a campaign against invented customers, grades the conversations that have finished, and proposes changes. If you let it, it also tests rewordings by itself.

<Note>
    **Three different things in Whappy use AI, and they do different jobs.**

    The **AI that talks to your leads** follows the funnel you built, asks your questions and books appointments. Everything under [Campaign](/campaign/context) configures it.

    The **Assistant** is the chat panel in the dashboard. It answers your questions and makes changes for you when you ask. See [Assistant limits](/autopilot/trust-levels).

    **Coach** and **Rehearsal** work on a campaign itself. They never talk to a real lead.
</Note>

## Switch the pages on per campaign

Coach and Rehearsal are optional pages. A campaign shows neither until you switch it on:

<Steps>
    <Step title="Open the campaign's Options">
        **Campaigns → your campaign → Options**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Find Sections">
        **Sections** lists the extra pages for this campaign's sidebar.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Switch on Coach, Rehearsal, or both">
        The change applies straight away. The page appears in the campaign's sidebar.
    </Step>
</Steps>

A campaign that talks to your existing customers (a [client campaign](/campaign/client-campaigns)) does not offer Coach or Rehearsal.

## Nothing changes by default

- Coach starts **Off** on every campaign. It still grades finished conversations, so when you switch it on it starts with what it already knows. It proposes nothing while it is off.
- Rehearsal runs only when you press **Rehearse**.
- Coach changes your wording by itself only if you set its mode to **Apply automatically**. Only you can choose that mode; the Assistant cannot choose it for you.

## How the pieces fit together

- Use **Rehearsal** before real leads arrive. Its list of the questions personas struggled with is the same signal Coach uses on real conversations.
- Use **Coach** once real conversations have finished. It tests a rewording against your original and keeps the better one.
- In **Apply automatically**, Coach only starts tests of rewordings by itself. Everything else waits for you. See [Coach](/autopilot/coach#the-three-modes).
