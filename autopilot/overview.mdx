Autopilot is the part of Whappy that works on your campaigns rather than on your leads. It can build a campaign, rehearse it against invented customers, watch how real conversations go, propose improvements, and — if you let it — apply them.

<Note>
    **Two different things are called "AI" in Whappy, and they do different jobs.**

    Your **AI assistant** talks to leads on WhatsApp. It follows the funnel you built, asks your questions, and books appointments. Everything under [Campaign](/campaign/context) configures it.

    **Autopilot** works on the campaign itself — building it, testing it, and improving it. It never talks to a lead. Everything on this tab configures Autopilot.
</Note>

## Nothing is on by default

A brand-new account starts with Autopilot at its lowest setting:

- Permissions are set to **Read only**. Autopilot can answer questions about your campaigns and change nothing.
- Coach is **Off** on every campaign. Conversations are still graded — that costs you nothing and builds up evidence — but no proposals are made.
- Rehearsal only runs when you press **Rehearse**.

You raise each of these when you are ready, one at a time.

## Permission is not the same as readiness

The two highest permission levels need more than your consent. You can grant **Launch**, but Autopilot stays at **Build** until your account has also demonstrated it is ready — three campaigns launched by hand without problems, and a rehearsal that passed.

This is deliberate. It means nobody reaches "the system messages real customers unattended" by clicking through a dialog on their first day. See [Permissions](/autopilot/trust-levels) for exactly what is required.

## How the pieces feed each other

Autopilot's parts are connected, and the connections are worth knowing:

- A **passing rehearsal** is one of the two things that unlocks the **Launch** permission level.
- **Coach** can only apply changes by itself when permissions are at **Operate** — the highest level.
- The **weekly briefing** reports what Autopilot did, including changes Coach kept and changes it reverted.
- If a watchdog notices something wrong — your WhatsApp sender quality falling, for instance — Autopilot is **paused automatically** back down to Build until you clear it.
