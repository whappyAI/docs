## From inside the app

The **Help** button in the left menu offers:

- **Take the tour**: a short walk through the app.
- **Contact support**: write your message and we reply by email. Mention the campaign and what you expected to happen.
- **Book a call**: opens our booking page.
- **Documentation**: opens these docs.

The **Assistant** panel in the dashboard can also answer questions about your account and tell you what a campaign is still missing. See [Assistant limits](/autopilot/trust-levels).

## Before contacting support

To help us help you faster:

<Steps>
    <Step title="Check the FAQ">
        Look through the [FAQ](/support/faq) for common questions.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check the campaign's own page">
        A campaign that can't start says why, with the first thing to fix. A message that wasn't delivered shows the reason in [Chats](/leads/conversation-history).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Gather the details">
        Have the error message and a screenshot or screen recording ready.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## What to include

- **Account**: the email address you sign in with.
- **Campaign**: its name.
- **What happened**: what you did, what you expected, and what you saw instead.
- **Error messages**: copied exactly.
- **Browser and device**.
- **Screenshots** or a screen recording.
