## Platform & Setup

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What is Whappy and how does it work?">
        Whappy is an AI assistant that talks to your leads and customers on WhatsApp and Instagram. You set it up in campaigns: you tell it about your business, choose what it asks each lead, and design the conversation as a funnel of steps. It asks your questions, answers theirs, books meetings and hands the conversation to you when you want. See [What is Whappy?](/introduction).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What are the system requirements to use Whappy?">
        - A modern web browser
        - A WhatsApp Business number, an Instagram professional account, or both. See [WhatsApp](/integrations/whatsapp) and [Instagram](/integrations/instagram)
        - An active plan for campaigns to run
        - Optional: a calendar (Calendly, Cal.com or GoHighLevel) for booking meetings, and Zapier, Make or a webhook to bring in leads
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How do I connect my WhatsApp Business account?">
        1. Go to **Integrations → WhatsApp**
        2. Press **Add new number**, or **Connect existing WhatsApp Business app** for a number you already use in the WhatsApp Business app
        3. In the Facebook window, log in, pick your Meta Business portfolio and WhatsApp Business account, and choose the number
        4. The number appears in the **Accounts** tab: no IDs or tokens to type. See [WhatsApp](/integrations/whatsapp)
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Funnel Configuration

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What is a funnel and how do I create one?">
        A funnel is the conversation your assistant follows with each lead, step by step. To build one, open a campaign and:

        1. Describe your business on the **Context** page
        2. Add the details to collect on the **Info** page
        3. On the **Funnel** page, press **Generate from context**, or add steps with **Add node** and connect them
        4. Press **Save changes**, then **Test**
        5. Set your language and working hours on the **Options** page

        See [Funnel](/campaign/funnel).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What types of nodes are available in the flow builder?">
        - **WhatsApp template**: the first message to a new lead (**Starts on Instagram** on an Instagram-only campaign)
        - **Ask information**: asks the lead for one of your Info fields
        - **Talk**: says something to the lead
        - **Support agent**: answers the lead's questions until an exit condition is met
        - **Send document**: sends PDFs or images from your [Assets](/campaign/assets)
        - **Send voice note**: sends a pre-recorded voice message
        - **Condition**: branches on a collected answer
        - **A/B test**: splits new leads between two paths and compares them
        - **Book meeting**: books on Calendly, Cal.com or GoHighLevel
        - **Send data**: sends what was collected to a webhook, Zapier or Make, or a WhatsApp alert
        - **Close**: ends the conversation, and can move the lead into another campaign

        On Instagram, documents and voice notes arrive as links. See [Funnel](/campaign/funnel#steps).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How do I set up conditional logic in my funnel?">
        1. Add a **Condition** step from **Add node**
        2. Under **Check if**, pick the Info field to check
        3. Choose the operator and the value: for a number, equals, greater than, less than and so on; for text, equals, contains, is empty, or a yes/no **Question**; for multiple choice, tick the options that count as true
        4. Under **If unknown**, choose which path to take when the answer is not known
        5. Connect the **True** and **False** outputs to the next steps

        See [Condition](/campaign/funnel#condition).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Can I collect custom information from leads?">
        Yes. On a campaign's **Info** page, add a field with **Generate with AI** or **Add manually**:

        - **Text**, **Number** or **Multiple choice**
        - Required (the assistant keeps asking) or optional (asked once)
        - Optional example questions to guide the assistant

        Then add an **Ask information** step for it in the funnel. See [Info](/campaign/info).
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Lead Management

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="How are leads added to my system?">
        - By hand, with **Add a lead** in the **⋮** menu of the campaign's Leads page
        - From a CSV, up to 10,000 rows per file. See [Adding & managing leads](/leads/lead-management)
        - From an integration: [Webhook](/integrations/webhook), [Zapier](/integrations/zapier), [Make](/integrations/make), [GoHighLevel](/integrations/gohighlevel), or a [Meta lead ad](/integrations/meta-conversions)
        - On Instagram, leads arrive when they write to you
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What's the difference between test and real leads?">
        - **Test leads** are created in the funnel's **Test** window. A test conversation never sends a message to a real phone, but a **Book meeting** step books real meetings, and **Send data** steps send their data unless **Skip test** is on.
        - **Real leads** get real messages from your assistant, and only they count on Home and in Analytics.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How can I view conversation history with leads?">
        Open **Chats** in the left menu and pick a conversation. You see every message, what was collected, and you can take over from the AI. From a campaign's Leads page, **View conversation** on a lead's row opens it too. See [Chats](/leads/conversation-history).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Can I manually send messages to leads?">
        Yes. Open **Chats**, select a conversation, and press **Stop AI conversation** to take over. The AI assistant stops replying and you can type directly in the message box.

        Press **Continue AI conversation** to hand it back. The lead sees one continuous WhatsApp conversation and cannot tell where the handover happened. See [Conversation history](/leads/conversation-history).
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Integrations

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What integrations does Whappy support?">
        - **Channels**: [WhatsApp](/integrations/whatsapp) and [Instagram](/integrations/instagram)
        - **Calendars**: [Calendly](/integrations/calendly), [Cal.com](/integrations/cal), [GoHighLevel](/integrations/gohighlevel)
        - **Leads and automation**: [Webhook](/integrations/webhook), [Zapier](/integrations/zapier), [Make](/integrations/make), [GoHighLevel](/integrations/gohighlevel)
        - **Ads**: [Meta Ads](/integrations/meta-conversions)
        - **Stores**: [Shopify](/integrations/shopify)
        - **AI clients**: [Claude and other MCP clients](/integrations/mcp)
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How do I set up Calendly integration?">
        Open **Integrations → Calendly** and connect your Calendly account. Its event types become available in the funnel's **Book meeting** step. See [Calendly](/integrations/calendly).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How does the Zapier integration work?">
        Use the Whappy app on Zapier to add leads to Whappy from forms, ads, sheets or any other app, and to start a Zap when something happens in a conversation, such as a conversation closing or a **Send data** step. [Make](/integrations/make) offers the same. See [Zapier](/integrations/zapier).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What data format should I use for webhook integrations?">
        Send a POST request with the lead's name, phone and any other details as JSON, with your API key in the `X-API-Key` header. The endpoint and key are under **Integrations → Webhook**. See [Webhook](/integrations/webhook) for the exact format and examples.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Campaign Management

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="How do I start and stop my campaign?">
        On the **Campaigns** page, use the switch on the campaign's row. The switch stays off while the campaign is not ready: the **Ready** column says what to fix first.

        While a campaign is stopped, nothing goes out and the assistant answers nobody. See [The campaigns list](/campaign/channel#the-campaigns-list).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What are operating hours and how do they work?">
        Working hours are set per campaign on its **Options** page: a time zone, and the days and hours.

        - New contacts and follow-ups go out only in these hours
        - Replies go out any time: a lead who writes at night gets an answer straight away
        - Appointment reminders go out on time, whatever the hour

        See [Options](/campaign/options#working-hours).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Can I run multiple campaigns simultaneously?">
        Yes. The **Campaigns** page lists all your campaigns, each with its own funnel and leads, and each can be started and stopped on its own.

        From a row's menu you can **Rename**, **Duplicate** (not campaigns for existing customers), **Set as default** and **Delete** (not while it runs).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What does the Ready column mean?">
        Whether the campaign can start. A green tick means it can; an amber triangle means it can't, and hovering it shows the first thing to fix:

        - **No active subscription**
        - **WhatsApp not connected**
        - **Instagram not connected**
        - **First message has no number**
        - **Funnel incomplete**
        - **Needs attention — open it**: something else, explained on the campaign's own page

        See [The campaigns list](/campaign/channel#the-campaigns-list).
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Technical & Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Why isn't my WhatsApp integration working?">
        - Open **Integrations → WhatsApp** and check that the number is still connected
        - Check that the template in your funnel's first step is still approved
        - Open a failed message in **Chats**: it shows the reason Meta gave
        - If a number was disconnected, reconnect it. See [WhatsApp](/integrations/whatsapp)
        - Contact support if it persists
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="My funnel isn't saving changes. What should I do?">
        - Check your internet connection and try **Save changes** again
        - Open **Funnel issues** on the canvas: it lists steps that are not configured
        - On a Condition step, check that a field, an operator and a value are set
        - If the save says an A/B test was ended elsewhere, reload the page
        - Reload the page and try again
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How do I troubleshoot appointment scheduling issues?">
        1. Check that your calendar is connected under **Integrations**
        2. Check that the event type is active in your calendar and has free slots
        3. Open the **Book meeting** step and check its **Meeting type**
        4. Try it with a test conversation: it books a real meeting on your calendar
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What should I do if leads aren't receiving messages?">
        Verify these settings:

        - Campaign is running (green status on home page)
        - Lead phone numbers are in correct international format (+1234567890)
        - Operating hours are configured and current time is within them
        - The WhatsApp number is still connected under **Integrations → WhatsApp**, and Meta has cleared it to send
        - Open the conversation in **Chats**: a failed message shows the reason Meta gave and how to fix it
        - Lead is not marked as a test lead (unless testing)
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How can I test my funnel before going live?">
        1. Save the funnel, then press **Test** on the canvas
        2. Pick a test lead or **Add a test lead**, and press **Start**
        3. Reply as the lead would. You see what the assistant says and what it collects
        4. Press **Restart** to try another path

        A test never messages a real phone. For a wider check, switch on [Rehearsal](/autopilot/rehearsal) in **Options → Sections**.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## API & Development

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Does Whappy provide an API for custom integrations?">
        Yes. Send leads to Whappy through the [Webhook](/integrations/webhook) endpoint with your API key, receive data from the funnel with a **Send data** step, or connect an AI client through [MCP](/integrations/mcp).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How do I handle webhook authentication?">
        Every request needs your API key in the `X-API-Key` header:

        ```
        X-API-Key: YOUR_API_KEY
        ```

        The key is under **Integrations → Webhook**. Anyone with it can add leads to your account: keep it on your server. See [Webhook](/integrations/webhook).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Can I export my lead data?">
        Yes. On a campaign's Leads tab, open the actions menu and choose **Export to CSV**. The export contains the leads currently listed, including the information collected from them, so filters and search narrow the export too.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing & Limits

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What are the usage limits for different plans?">
        Plans limit how many new leads you can start per day and per month. Home shows how many leads you have used this month out of your limit. See **Plans** in the account menu for the plans.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How is usage calculated?">
        Each new lead whose conversation starts counts once. The monthly count follows your billing period.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my plan limits?">
        New leads are not started until the limit resets or you upgrade. Conversations already going continue. Upgrade under **Plans** in the account menu.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security & Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="How does Whappy protect my data and my leads' data?">
        - All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
        - Data is processed under the GDPR; a [Data Processing Agreement](https://whappy.ai/legal/dpa) is available
        - Lead data is never sold. It is shared only with the services that make Whappy work, such as Meta for WhatsApp and Instagram and our cloud and payment providers, as listed in the [Privacy Policy](https://whappy.ai/legal/privacy)
        - Access to your account is authenticated on every request, and the assistant's action log deliberately stores field names only, never their values
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Can I delete lead data?">
        Yes. On a campaign's Leads page, delete one lead with the bin button on its row, or tick several and use **Delete selected**. Their conversations are kept. For account data removal, [contact support](/support/contact-support).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
        Whappy runs on Google Cloud, with its services deployed in European regions. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your information.

        If you need a specific written commitment on data residency — for a procurement review or a DPA — [contact support](/support/contact-support) and we will confirm the current arrangement for your account in writing.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
