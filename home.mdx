Home is the first page after you log in. It is one column of plain rows: what needs a person, the last 30 days as a chart, your campaigns with their numbers, which integrations are connected, and your plan in one line. Nothing is switched on or off from here; every row opens the place where you act.

The title is a short greeting that changes from visit to visit. The numbers only count real leads and real chats.

## Needs you

The first block lists what is waiting for a person, one row each, with a link on the right. When nothing is waiting, it reads **Nothing needs you right now.**

| Row | Meaning | Where it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **N unread chats waiting for an answer** | Chats nobody on your team has read yet. | **Open chats** takes you to Chats. |
| **Campaign can't start: reason** | A campaign that is not running and cannot be started as it is. The reason is the first thing to fix. | **Open** takes you to the campaign. |
| **Integration needs attention** | A connected integration that Whappy has flagged. | **Open** takes you to the integration's page. |

A running campaign never appears here: it is already talking. The reasons a campaign can show are:

- **No active subscription**
- **WhatsApp not connected**
- **Instagram not connected**
- **First message has no number**: the account has a WhatsApp number, but this campaign's first message does not name one.
- **Funnel incomplete**
- **Needs attention — open it**: something else, explained on the campaign's own page.

## Last 30 days

Three lines, one point per day, oldest day on the left:

- **Chats started**: chats that opened that day.
- **Chats closed**: chats that reached the end of the funnel that day.
- **Appointments**: appointments booked that day.

Hover the chart for the three values on one day.

<Note>
    "Chats closed" here is the same thing the [Analytics](/analytics) page calls **Qualified** and the lead table calls **Completed**: a conversation that reached the end of the funnel.
</Note>

## Campaigns

Your campaigns as a table, with **N of M live** after the title and **All campaigns** on the right, which opens the Campaigns page.

The first row, in bold, is **All campaigns**: the total leads and chats across every campaign. Under it, one row per campaign with its channel icons, its name, its **Leads** and its **Chats**. A zero is shown as **—**. A row opens the campaign.

Campaigns are ordered by what they need from you: campaigns that cannot start come first, then the live ones, then the paused ones.

## Integrations

Every integration your account is offered, as rows with its logo, and **N connected** after the title. **All integrations** on the right opens the Integrations page.

Each row ends with its state:

- **Connected**
- **Needs attention**: Whappy has flagged it; the same integration appears under **Needs you**.
- **Connect**: not connected yet. Its logo is greyed out.

Connected and flagged integrations are listed first. A row opens the integration's page.

## Plan

One line at the bottom, shown only while you have an active subscription: the plan's name, how many leads you have used this month out of your limit, and when the plan renews. A thin bar under it shows how much of the month's leads you have used. **Plan** on the right opens your plan settings.

<Note>
    An agency account working as itself has no campaigns or leads of its own: instead of Home, it lands on its client list. Open a client to see that client's Home.
</Note>
