**Context** is what your assistant knows about your business. A campaign opens on this page. On a [campaign for existing customers](/campaign/client-campaigns) the same page is called **Knowledge**.

The page has three sections, top to bottom: the assistant's name, your sources, and the Business Context written from them. Press **Save** at the bottom to keep your changes, or **Reset** to throw them away.

## Assistant name

The name the assistant introduces itself with, up to 30 characters.

**Examples:** `Sarah from TechCorp`, `Mike - Web Design`, `Lisa | Marketing Agency`

## Sources

Web pages, documents and notes the assistant learns your business from. Only their text is kept.

Press **Add source** and pick one:

| Option | What it does | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add web page** | Reads one page at the address you give. Add other pages one by one. | 8 pages |
| **Upload document** | Reads a price list, a brochure, a menu: PDF, Word, photo, spreadsheet or text, up to 15 MB. The file itself is not kept, only its text. | 10 documents |
| **Write a note** | Anything the assistant should know that your pages and documents don't say, for example your opening hours. Up to 20,000 characters. | 20 notes |

On a campaign for existing customers, the menu also offers **Import from another campaign**: it copies what the assistant knows from another campaign. Items with the same name are replaced; offers stay.

Each source shows how many characters of text it gave. A very long source is marked *shortened to fit*. A web page that could not be read says why, for example that the page couldn't be reached or has almost no readable text.

Next to each source:

- **Read again** (web pages): reads the page again, for when your site has changed.
- **Edit note** (notes).
- **Remove source**. The Business Context keeps what it learned from it until you update the context again.

<Note>
    Adding or removing a source does not change what the assistant knows yet. **Update context** does.
</Note>

## Business Context

What the assistant reads before every message. It is written from your sources, and you can edit it as you like, up to 16,000 characters.

Press **Update context** to write it from your sources. The button needs at least one source that could be read. After an update, the page may tell you:

- **Not in your sources**: things the assistant may be asked about that your sources don't answer. Add them to a source and update again.
- That your sources say more than fits, so the least important details were left out.
- Later, when you add or change a source: *Your sources changed since the last update.* Update the context so the assistant knows.

If you edited the Business Context yourself, **Update context** first asks **Replace the current text?** Choose **Keep my version** to stop, or **Replace** to write a new version from your sources.

<Tip>
    Changes apply from the assistant's next message, in conversations already going as well as new ones.
</Tip>

## The campaign language

The language the assistant writes in is not set here. It is on the campaign's [Options](/campaign/options#language) page.
