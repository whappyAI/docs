Most campaigns go out to people who have never bought from you. This one is for the people who already have.

A campaign for existing customers keeps **one conversation per customer, open for good**. There is no funnel to build and no point at which the conversation is finished. Your customer writes whenever they want something, the assistant answers using what it knows about your business, and when you have an offer, you send it into a conversation that already exists.

<Note>
    You choose the kind of campaign when you create it, and **it can't be changed afterwards**. The two work so differently that converting one into the other would lose either the funnel or the conversation history.
</Note>

## Creating one

On the **Campaigns** page, press **New campaign**. Under **What is this campaign for?**, choose **Talk to existing customers** (the other choice is **Find new customers**).

The choice is not offered on an account that has only Instagram connected: these campaigns write to customers on WhatsApp.

The campaign has different pages from a campaign for new customers:

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Knowledge** | What the assistant knows about your business: what you sell, your hours, your policies. The same page as [Context](/campaign/context). |
| **Files** | Documents, images and voice notes the assistant may send when the conversation calls for them. Shown once you switch it on under Settings → **Sections**. See [Assets](/campaign/assets#files-the-ai-may-send-on-its-own). |
| **Offers** | What you want your customers to hear about. See [Offers](/campaign/offers). |
| **Clients** | The customers this campaign talks to, and who asked not to receive promotions. |
| **Announcements** | Sending an offer out to customers. See [Announcements](/campaign/announcements). |
| **Settings** | The number it sends from, language, pacing, the welcome and working hours. See [Options](/campaign/options). |

There is no Funnel page and no Info page: the conversation and its questions are generated from each offer.

### The set-up checklist

Until the campaign is set up, a checklist sits at the top of each of its pages, headed **Set-up: N of 6 done**:

1. **Choose the number**
2. **Tell the assistant about you**
3. **Add your customers**
4. **Write an offer**
5. **Announce it**
6. **Start the campaign**

Each step links to the page where it is done and is ticked off as you go. **Start the campaign** has its own **Start** button. The checklist disappears once everything is ticked.

A new campaign opens on **Settings**, because the first thing to do is choose which of your WhatsApp numbers it sends from: your approved templates belong to that number. Once the number is set, the campaign opens on **Knowledge**.

## Before you start

You need a **connected WhatsApp number** and at least one **approved WhatsApp template**. Without a number, every page of the campaign says so, with **Connect WhatsApp**.

A template is an opening message WhatsApp has approved in advance. It is required to write to a customer who has not written to you in the last 24 hours. That rule is WhatsApp's. Once the customer replies, the assistant writes freely.

One approved template is enough to start. You don't need a different one for every offer.

## Importing your customers

On the **Clients** page, open the **⋮** menu and choose **Import from CSV**.

The file needs a **phone** column. **name**, **tags** and **language** are optional; every other column becomes a fact the assistant knows about that customer.

```
phone,name,tags,plan
+393331234567,Marco,Premium,Annual
+393339876543,Giulia,Milan,Monthly
```

- If your file has local numbers without a country code, set the **Default country code**.
- A file can have up to 10,000 rows. Your plan sets how many customers you can add each month, and the import tells you if the file is over it. An active plan is needed.
- Before the import runs you confirm: *These customers agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from my business.* This is not a formality. Importing a list that did not agree is how a number gets reported and blocked.

The summary says how many were **imported**, **updated** and **skipped**. You can download the skipped rows to see why. Someone already on your list is updated, not duplicated, and is not messaged again.

The same menu offers **Import from another campaign**: it brings people over from another of your campaigns. They start a fresh conversation, and anyone who opted out stays opted out.

## The welcome

You can set a message the assistant sends to new customers when they are imported. **It is optional**: without it, imported customers wait quietly until you announce an offer.

In **Settings**, under **How new customers are welcomed**, press **Set up a welcome**:

- **What should it say?**: describe it in plain words, for example *Say hi from Bar Rossi, mention we're open until 8pm, and that they can order here any time.*
- **Opening message**: the approved template it opens with.
- **Anything to ask?**: optional questions, pressing Enter after each. If someone would rather not answer, the assistant moves on and doesn't ask again.

### Why the welcome takes days

A large import does not go out in an afternoon. It goes out a few at a time, only during your working hours, at the pace set in **Settings**.

That pace is the protection. Sending thousands of messages from a number in one hour is the fastest way to have WhatsApp flag it, and a flagged number stops working for everybody, including the customers already talking to you.

The **Clients** page shows **Welcoming new customers**: how many have been welcomed out of how many, and roughly when it will finish.

If you need a specific customer reached sooner, message them yourself from [Chats](/leads/conversation-history). The assistant picks the conversation up from there.

## Labels

Labels are how an announcement picks its audience: `Premium`, `Milan`, `lapsed`. They arrive with a CSV `tags` column, and you can set them on the **Clients** page.

- **One customer**: the **Labels** column on their row.
- **Several at once**: tick the customers and use **Add label** or **Remove label** in the bar that appears.
- **All of them**: **Manage labels** in the **⋮** menu lists every label with how many customers carry it. Renaming one renames it for all of them; renaming it to a label that already exists merges the two. Deleting one removes it from all of them. Nobody is deleted, only the label.

Labels belong to the campaign. An announcement already sent keeps the name it used.

## When a customer asks to stop

A customer who asks to stop receiving promotions is marked **Opted out** on the **Clients** page, and no further offers or announcements reach them.

They are **not** cut off. They can still message you, and the assistant still answers them, still helps them, still knows who they are. Opting out ends the marketing, not the service.

## What happens next

Once your customers are in, [write an offer](/campaign/offers) and the assistant can start telling them about it.
