After you sign up you land on [Home](/home). At the foot of the left menu, a **Setup** card shows how far you are (for example *Setup · 1 of 4*) and the next step. Click it to unfold the checklist; each step opens the page where it is done. **Show me around** starts a short tour of the app. The card disappears once you have a lead and a way to talk to them.

The steps:

1. **Build conversation**
2. **Add knowledge** (optional)
3. **Connect WhatsApp** (or **Connect WhatsApp or Instagram**, on accounts where Instagram is offered)
4. **Go live**
5. **First lead**

<Note>
    An account set up from a Shopify store has its own list: **Connect your store**, **Connect WhatsApp**, **Choose a plan** and **Switch on your campaigns**. See [Shopify](/integrations/shopify).
</Note>

## 1. Create a campaign

A campaign is one assistant talking to one group of leads. Open **Campaigns** and press **New campaign** (or **Create your first campaign**). Give it a name. Depending on your account you are also asked:

- **What is this campaign for?**: **Find new customers**, the campaign this guide follows, or **Talk to existing customers**. See [Campaigns for existing customers](/campaign/client-campaigns).
- **Where does it talk?**: WhatsApp, Instagram or both. See [Channel](/campaign/channel).

The campaign opens on its **Context** page.

## 2. Tell the assistant about your business

On [Context](/campaign/context), set the **Assistant name**, then press **Add source** to add your website, a document such as a price list, or a note. Press **Update context**: Whappy writes the **Business Context** the assistant reads before every message. Read it, correct it, and press **Save**.

## 3. Choose what to collect

On [Info](/campaign/info), add the details the assistant should find out from each lead, such as a budget or a timeline. **Generate with AI** creates a field from a sentence; **Add manually** lets you fill it in.

## 4. Build the conversation

On [Funnel](/campaign/funnel), press **Generate from context** to have a flow designed from your Business Context, or build it yourself with **Add node**. The first step is the WhatsApp template that opens the conversation; every path ends with a Close step. Press **Save changes**.

Press **Test** to play the lead in a test conversation. It never messages a real phone, but a **Book meeting** step books real meetings on your calendar.

<Tip>
    For a wider check, switch on **Rehearsal** under the campaign's **Options → Sections**: [Rehearsal](/autopilot/rehearsal) runs six invented customers through the campaign. Nothing is sent to anybody.
</Tip>

## 5. Set the language and hours

On [Options](/campaign/options), pick the **Campaign language**, then your **Time zone** and **Days and hours**. New contacts and follow-ups go out only in these hours; replies go out any time. Press **Save**.

## 6. Connect WhatsApp or Instagram

- **WhatsApp**: open [Integrations → WhatsApp](/integrations/whatsapp) and connect your number through Facebook. Then add a message template and wait for Meta to approve it: on WhatsApp, the first message to a new lead must be an approved template. Pick it in the funnel's first step.
- **Instagram**: open [Integrations → Instagram](/integrations/instagram). On Instagram people write to you first, so there is no template to approve.

## 7. Go live

Open **Campaigns** and turn on the campaign's switch. The switch stays off while the campaign is not ready: the **Ready** column shows a green tick when it can start, or an amber triangle with the first thing to fix. See [The campaigns list](/campaign/channel#the-campaigns-list).

A running campaign answers people who write to you. It needs an active plan: see **Plans** in the account menu.

## 8. Get your first lead

- **WhatsApp**: on the campaign's **Leads** page, open the **⋮** menu and choose **Add a lead** or **Import from CSV**, or connect a lead source such as a form, your CRM or a [webhook](/integrations/webhook). See [Adding & managing leads](/leads/lead-management).
- **Instagram**: your first lead arrives when someone writes to you on Instagram.

Follow the conversations in [Chats](/leads/conversation-history), where you can also take over from the AI. Home shows what [needs you](/home#needs-you).
